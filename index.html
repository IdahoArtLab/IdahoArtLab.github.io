Your Creative Hub

The Idaho Art Lab is a non-profit, volunteer-run makerspace. Our mission is simple: to make creative exploration accessible to everyone in our community.

From the moment you step through the doors, you'll feel part of a community passionate about making. Whether you're picking up a paintbrush, prototyping on a 3D printer, or making your first animation, the Idaho Art Lab is where inspiration meets innovation.

Explore The Labs

Ceramics Painting Studio

Unleash your creativity on hundreds of ceramic bisque ware pieces. Access our extensive 1,500+ casting mold inventory.

Pottery Wheel & Kiln Studio

Work on one of our 11 electric pottery wheels, kick wheel, slab roller, or extruder. We offer firing services up to Cone 10.

Sculpture Lab

Utilize our ergonomic electric sculptor's tables or sculpture stands to elevate your projects. Clay for sale and firing services available.

Drawing & Painting Studio

An inspiring space, free and open to the public. Access our light table, easels, projector, paper cutters, and large project tables.

Animation Lab

Explore traditional animation with a light table and copy stand, or rent a complete Digital Cartoon Animation Station.

Lapidary Studio

Transform raw stones into polished works of art with equipment for stone slabbing, trimming, grinding, and polishing.

3D Print Lab

Bring your digital designs to life with our FDM printers for rapid prototyping, artistic creations, and functional parts.

Lost Wax Metal Casting

Craft intricate jewelry or small sculptures in metal using our 6 jewelers benches and high-quality centrifuge casting method.

Screen Printing Studio

Produce fine art serigraphs or custom apparel using our photo emulsion tables, vacuum table, and a 4-color T-Shirt press.

Block & Monoprinting

Explore printmaking by carving linoleum blocks, or experiment with monoprinting using our brayers and gelatin plates.

Digital Art & Media Lab

Dive into digital creation with powerful workstations for Photoshop, 3D Rendering, a green screen, and a sound booth.

Photography Darkroom

Develop your own black and white photography in our large format darkroom. Supports developing negatives up to 4" x 5".

Vacuum Forming & Vinyl Baking

Form plastic, silicone, or vinyl sheets up to 4x4 feet with our vacuum former, or bake vinyl onto metal for durable finishes.

Podcasting and Video Studio

Record, edit, and produce high-quality audio and video content for podcasting, interviews, and green-screen production.

Become a Member

Unlock your creative potential with 24/7 makerspace access and join a vibrant community of fellow artists and makers. Here’s what you get and how to start.

Key Benefits

  • Full 24/7 Access to the Makerspace
  • Access to Over $100,000 in Tools and Equipment
  • Free or Discounted Project Supplies
  • Exclusive Members-Only Events
  • Personal Project Storage Space
  • Your space! Change, modify and improve the space.

Get Started in 5 Steps

  1. Attend a Tour & Induction (Tues/Thurs at 7pm).
  2. Complete the required tool safety training.
  3. Sign the liability waiver and membership agreement.
  4. Pay a one-time $35 induction fee.
  5. Set up your $125 monthly membership.

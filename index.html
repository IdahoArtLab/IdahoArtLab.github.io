Your Creative Hub in Idaho

The Idaho Art Lab is a thriving non-profit, volunteer-run makerspace. Our mission is simple: to make creative exploration accessible to everyone in our community.

From the moment you step through our doors, you'll feel part of a community passionate about making. Whether you're picking up a paintbrush, firing up a 3D printer, or coding your first animation, the Idaho Art Lab is where inspiration meets innovation.

Explore The Studios & Labs

Ceramics Painting Studio

Unleash your creativity on hundreds of ceramic bisque ware pieces. Access our extensive 1,500+ casting mold inventory to find the perfect piece to paint or glaze.

Pottery Wheel & Kiln Studio

Work on one of our 11 electric pottery wheels, adult kick wheel, slab roller, or extruder. We offer firing services up to Cone 10 in electric or gas kilns, plus a raku kiln. Clay is available for purchase.

Sculpture Lab

Utilize our ergonomic electric sculptor's tables (3) or sculpture stands (6) to elevate your projects. We provide 5 lb. bags of clay for sale and offer firing services by-the-inch or full kiln rental.

Drawing & Painting Studio

An inspiring space, free and open to the public. Access our 4 ft. light table, straight-edge boards, various easels, overhead projector, paper cutters, and large project tables. Bring your ideas to life!

Animation Lab

Explore traditional animation with a large light table, copy stand, and computer digitizing station. For advanced projects, rent a complete Digital Cartoon Animation Station and bring your characters to life.

Lapidary Studio

Transform raw stones into polished works of art. Our lapidary lab features equipment for stone slabbing, trimming, grinding, and polishing, perfect for creating beautiful cabochons and finished gems.

3D Print Lab

Bring your digital designs to life! Our 3D print lab offers several FDM printers for rapid prototyping, artistic creations, and functional parts. Learn to design for 3D printing and turn your ideas into physical objects.

Lost Wax Metal Casting

Craft intricate jewelry pieces or small sculptures in metal using our specialized equipment. We offer 6 jewelers benches and utilize a high-quality centrifuge casting method for superior results.

Screen Printing Studio

Produce professional fine art serigraphs or custom apparel using our photo emulsion tables, a 4 ft. vacuum table, over 90 screens, and a 4-color T-Shirt press. Your designs, our tools!

Block & Monoprinting

Explore printmaking by carving into linoleum blocks, or experiment with unique monoprinting techniques using our brayers, glass surfaces, book press, and Speedball gelatin plates.

Digital Art & Media Lab

Dive into digital creation with our powerful workstations. Learn software like Photoshop, Adobe Creative Suite, and 3D Rendering. We also offer a 10 ft. green screen for video production and a sound booth for professional voice-overs.

Photography Darkroom

Step back in time and develop your own black and white photography. Our large format darkroom supports developing negatives up to 4" x 5" and printing up to 16" x 20".

Vacuum Forming & Vinyl Baking

Create custom shapes and durable finishes. Form plastic, silicone, or vinyl sheets up to 4x4 feet with our vacuum former. You can also bake vinyl onto metal for long-lasting, vibrant custom pieces.

Podcasting and Video Studio

Record, edit, and produce high-quality audio and video content. Our studio is equipped for podcasting, interviews, green-screen video production, and more. Share your stories with the world!

Become a Member

Unlock your creative potential with 24/7 access to our labs and equipment. A membership is the most affordable way to use our facility, get discounts on classes, and join a vibrant community of fellow artists and makers.

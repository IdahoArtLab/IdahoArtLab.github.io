A Creative Hub for Everyone

The Idaho Art Lab promotes the visual arts and provides opportunities to people of any age, ability or economic status to engage in the arts by offering art education programs, gallery exhibits & a gathering place for residents to be involved in the arts as part of their daily life.

The Place to Be for Visual Arts

The Idaho Art Lab is a non-profit, volunteer-run makerspace dedicated to giving our community a place to learn and create. From ceramics to digital design, we provide affordable access to tools and knowledge.

Our mission is to make the arts a part of daily life for everyone. Whether you're a seasoned artist or a curious beginner, you'll find a welcoming space and a supportive community here.

Explore the Labs

Ceramics Studio

We offer hundreds of ceramic bisque ware to paint or glaze. The ~1,500+ casting molds inventory is in a database so you have access to ALL of our inventory, not just the bisque ware we have room to display.

Pottery Studio

We have 11 electric pottery wheels, 1 adult kick wheel, a slab roller & extruder. We can fire to Cone 10 with electric or gas kilns and have a raku kiln in the kiln shelter. We sell clay in 5 lb. bags.

Sculpture Lab

Use one of the 3 ergonomic electric sculptor's tables or 6 sculpture stands to raise & lower your sculpture base. We sell clay in 5 lb. bags and can fire your works by-the-inch or kiln rental.

Drawing & Painting

Free and open to the public. Use our 4 ft. light table, a straight-edge board, easels (standing, bench or tabletop), overhead projector, paper cutters and large project tables.

Cartoon Animation

A large light table is set up next to a copy stand and computer digitizing station. You can also rent a complete Digital Cartoon Animation Station.

Lapidary

Our lapidary lab includes equipment for stone slabbing, trimming, grinding, and polishing to create beautiful cabochons and finished stones.

Faceting

Let our Faceter guide you in cutting & polishing facets (angle cuts) into precious gemstones or glass to bring out their inner brilliance.

Lost Wax Metal Casting

Create intricate jewelry or small sculptures in metal. We have 6 jewelers benches and use a centrifuge method for high-quality casting.

Screen Printing

Featuring photo emulsion tables, a 4 ft. vacuum table, 90+ screens, and a 4-color T-Shirt press for fine art serigraphs or apparel.

Block & Monoprinting

Carve into lino blocks, use our brayers, glass surfaces, and book press. You can also try out Speedball gelatin plate printing for unique monoprints.

Computer & Media Lab

We teach Photoshop, Adobe Creative Suite, and 3D Rendering. A 10 ft. green screen is available for video, plus a sound booth for voice-overs.

Photography Darkroom

Our large format photography darkroom offers developing of black & white negatives up to 4" x 5" and prints up to 16" x 20".

Vacuum Forming / Vinyl Baking

Form plastic, silicone, or vinyl sheets up to 4x4 feet. You can also bake vinyl onto metal for durable, custom finishes.

Wood Working

Our wood shop is equipped for detailed carving and cutting. We have a scroll saw, hand saws, and flex shafts available for wood carving projects.

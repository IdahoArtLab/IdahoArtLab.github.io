Welcome!

The Idaho Art Lab is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit makerspace located in St. Anthony, Idaho. We provide residents and rural creatives with access to art studios, fabrication labs, and learning opportunities—empowering lifelong creativity through affordable tools and training.

Studios & Labs

Ceramics Studio

We offer hundreds of ceramic bisque ware to paint or glaze. Access our 1,500+ casting molds inventory through our digital database.

Pottery Studio

11 electric wheels, 1 adult kick wheel, slab roller & extruder. Fire to Cone 10 with electric/gas kilns. Clay: $5/5lb bag.

Sculpture Lab

3 ergonomic electric sculptor's tables & 6 adjustable stands. Clay available ($5/5lb). Kiln rental by inch or full load.

Drawing & Painting

Free public access with 4ft light table, easels, projectors, and large format paper cutters. Open daily 9AM-9PM.

Cartoon Animation

Light table, copy stand, and digital stations. Rent animation software packages hourly or weekly.

Lapidary

Slabbing saws, trim saws & polishing wheels. Create cabochons from local stones or imported materials.

Faceting

Guided sessions on Ultra Tec faceting machines. Bring your own gemstones or use our practice materials.

Lost Wax Casting

Centrifuge casting station with 6 jewelers benches. Bronze, silver & aluminum alloys available.

Screen Printing

90 screens & 4-color T-shirt press. Photo emulsion setup for detailed serigraphs. Bring your own shirts or paper.

Block Printing

Linoleum blocks, brayers & book press. Try gelatin plate monoprinting with our Speedball setup.

Computer Arts Lab

Adobe Creative Cloud, 3D rendering & video editing. Includes 10ft green screen & sound booth.

Photography Darkroom

Process 4x5" negatives & print up to 16x20". Chemistry provided - bring your own paper or purchase onsite.

Vacuum Forming

Form plastic/silicone up to 4x4ft. Bake vinyl onto metal for signs & functional art pieces.

Wood Working

Scroll saw, hand tools & flex shafts. Stock wood available or bring your own materials for carving.
